Models
------

ggml-small-q5_1.bin
  Whisper "small" speech recognition model by OpenAI (MIT), converted to
  the ggml format by the whisper.cpp project.
  https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp

ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin
  Silero VAD voice activity detection model by the Silero team (MIT),
  converted to the ggml format by the whisper.cpp project.
  https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/whisper-vad

opus-mt-fr-en, opus-mt-en-fr, opus-mt-fr-ru, opus-mt-en-ru, opus-mt-ru-fr
  OPUS-MT translation models by the Language Technology Research Group at
  the University of Helsinki (Apache-2.0), converted to the CTranslate2
  format with 8-bit weights for LiveSubtitles.
  https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-fr-en
  https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-fr
  https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-fr-ru
  https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-ru
  https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-ru-fr

opus-mt-ru-en
  OPUS-MT translation model by the Language Technology Research Group at
  the University of Helsinki, licensed under the Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International license (CC-BY-4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified for LiveSubtitles:
  converted to the CTranslate2 format with 8-bit weights.
  https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-ru-en
